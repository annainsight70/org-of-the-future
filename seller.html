01Question one

Why Agent Store?

Watch Tutorial on Agent Store

Insight's Agent Store is the front door to the best of our artifacts and capabilities as an AI Solutions Integrator. 

ONE THING TO REMEMBER

AI budgets increasingly live outside of IT and procurement. To drive value to non-traditional buyers and lines of business, we will pilot consistent, proven, and repeatable AI solutions to open the door.

Every solution supports an "art of the possible" experience for clients.

What it is

  • Agent Store packages proven delivery artifacts and our "Client Zero" projects into a customer-facing catalog.
  • Sold projects should drive products, licensing, and services upsell.

What it is not

  • Not a transactable, reseller catalog. Insight builds and deploys these solutions for clients.
  • Not licenced per-user. Agents and solutions run in the client's own tenant on their infrastructure.

THE MARKET REALITY

What's a "door crasher"?

Try with the Pre-Sales Opportunity Agent
A door crasher is a concrete, compelling solution that earns us a conversation with a senior business leader. 

Instead of opening with meetings with: "Would you like to discuss licensing or hardware?",
open with: "How do your business units handle delayed orders, staff turnover, policy gaps, or AI governance risks?"

We try not to price doorknobs. Once the client engages, we earn the right to their larger transformation roadmap.
    • Step 1Open conversations with a business focus. Identify opportunities that resonate & demo solutions from Agent Store.
    • Step 2Qualify the client's business problem. Bring in the appropriate pre-sales team to design and close the first engagement.
    • Step 3Prove value and upsell to DevShop or additional, enhanced offerings.
